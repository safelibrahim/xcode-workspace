# Apple Media Apps & Now Playing Surfaces — Visual Design Patterns for a Compact Notch "Now Playing" Module

Research date: 2026-10-05. Scope: Apple Music (macOS mini player / full player), Control Center & menu bar Now Playing (macOS), Apple TV app, macOS 26 Tahoe / iOS 26 Liquid Glass redesign (and a note on macOS 27).

**Source-access caveat (important for the report writer):** From this research environment, only developer.apple.com could be fetched directly. apple.com/newsroom, support.apple.com, 9to5Mac, Six Colors, and MacStories were blocked by the egress proxy, so claims from those sites come from **search-result snippets** rather than full-page reads. I found **no published pixel measurements** of the Mac Music mini player or the Control Center Now Playing module from Apple or from credible reviewers. Every numeric value below is labeled one of three ways:
- **[Documented]**: from Apple HIG or developer documentation.
- **[Reported]**: from third-party reporting, read only as a search snippet.
- **[Estimate]**: my own inference or memory of the UI. It was not verified against a source in this session.

## 1. Mac Music mini player and Control Center / menu bar Now Playing: layout, artwork, typography, controls, scrubber

### Takeaway
Apple documents behavior, not metrics, for these surfaces. The MiniPlayer is artwork-dominant. Its transport controls and (since 26.2) its track title show up only on hover. A "Hide Artwork" mode turns it into a compact text-and-controls strip. The Control Center / menu bar Now Playing module shows artwork, title, artist, and previous/play-pause/next. Apple's closest documented analog for a black notch module is the Dynamic Island Live Activity spec: a black opaque background, a 44 pt corner radius, concentric margins, and medium-or-heavier text. Use that, together with the macOS text-style table, as the numeric foundation.

### Cited Findings
- MiniPlayer (Apple Support, covers macOS Tahoe 26 and Sequoia 15): "The main part of the window shows the album artwork for the song that's playing, with a slider to move to a different part of the song, and buttons to adjust the volume, show lyrics, and open the queue." [Reported via snippet] — [Apple Support: Use Music MiniPlayer on Mac](https://support.apple.com/guide/music/use-music-miniplayer-mus71d7dcfce/mac)
- MiniPlayer has a "Hide Artwork" option in the More menu that switches it to a smaller window without art, and "Show Artwork" restores it. A "Keep MiniPlayer on top of all other windows" setting lives in Music > Settings > Advanced. A song must be playing before you can use the MiniPlayer. [Reported via snippet] — [Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/guide/music/use-music-miniplayer-mus71d7dcfce/mac)
- "When you move the pointer over the artwork, controls appear." In macOS 26.2 the mini player "no longer displays the name of the track… except when you hover the mouse pointer over it." The "Hide Large Artwork" option keeps song info visible without hovering. [Reported, user forum] — [Apple Community thread 256229550](https://discussions.apple.com/thread/256229550)
- Tahoe full Music window: the playback controls and progress bar moved from the top of the window to the bottom, on a transparent Liquid Glass bar. Users complain that it "washes away" against white playlists and that album art scrolls behind it. [Reported, user forum] — [Apple Community 256142581](https://discussions.apple.com/thread/256142581); [Apple Community 256137017](https://discussions.apple.com/thread/256137017)
- Users say the MiniPlayer has no "add current track to library" button. [Reported] — [Apple Community 256135938](https://discussions.apple.com/thread/256135938)
- Menu bar / Control Center Now Playing: when it is playing, a small playback icon appears in the menu bar. Clicking it shows the track name, artist, album art, and transport controls (previous, play/pause, next). [Reported, low-authority blog] — [theodorehq.com](https://www.theodorehq.com/echo/blog/posts/show-now-playing-in-menu-bar-mac)
- In Tahoe the Now Playing module can be set to "Always Show", "Show When Active", or "Never Show" in the menu bar. [Reported] — [Macworld](https://www.macworld.com/article/2859555/how-to-take-full-control-of-your-control-center-in-macos-tahoe.html); [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/use-the-new-control-center-features-in-macos-26-tahoe/)
- Tahoe Control Center modules sit in a grid and can take 1 spot or up to 4 spots (a 2×2 square or a horizontal strip), modeled on iOS 18's Control Center. [Reported] — [9to5Mac (snippet)](https://9to5mac.com/2025/09/11/macos-tahoe-has-big-control-center-upgrades-heres-everything-new/); [Wikipedia: macOS Tahoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MacOS_Tahoe)
- Menu bar height: Sequoia is 25 px and Tahoe is 31 px, according to a forum post. [Reported, forum; unverified] — [MacRumors forum](https://forums.macrumors.com/threads/yet-another-stupid-thing-about-tahoe-new-ui-the-menu-bar-is-taller-for-no-reason.2465392/)
- macOS built-in text styles [Documented], given as style / weight / size pt / line height pt / emphasized weight: Large Title Regular 26/32 Bold; Title 1 Regular 22/26 Bold; Title 2 Regular 17/22 Bold; Title 3 Regular 15/20 Semibold; **Headline Bold 13/16 Heavy; Body Regular 13/16 Semibold; Callout Regular 12/15 Semibold; Subheadline Regular 11/14 Semibold; Footnote Regular 10/13 Semibold**; Caption 1 Regular 10/13 Medium; Caption 2 Medium 10/13 Semibold. — [HIG: Typography](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/typography)
- Dynamic Island (the closest analog to a notch HUD) [Documented]: "Live Activities in the Dynamic Island use a black opaque background. Consider using bold colors for text and objects…"; "The Dynamic Island uses a corner radius of 44 points"; "Use large, heavier-weight text — a medium weight or higher. Use small text sparingly"; "Use consistent margins and concentric placement… match its corner radius to the outer corner radius of the Live Activity by subtracting the margin"; "Keep content compact and snug within a margin that's concentric to the outer edge"; compact/minimal width is 230–250 pt and expanded width is 371–408 pt depending on device; the Lock Screen presentation is 371–408 × 84–160 pt; "Ensure unified information and design of the compact presentations… use consistent color and typography" across leading and trailing elements. — [HIG: Live Activities](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/live-activities)
- Apple says you don't control how the system formats Now Playing info: "You don't have direct control over what information the system displays, or its formatting… the system… handles displaying the information in a consistent manner for all apps." Apple asks you to provide as many metadata properties as possible (title, artist, album, artwork, elapsed time, duration, rate). — [MPNowPlayingInfoCenter docs](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/mediaplayer/mpnowplayinginfocenter)

### Inferences
- **[Estimate] Typography mapping for a notch module, built from the documented macOS styles:** title = 13 pt semibold (Body, emphasized); artist and album = 11–12 pt regular in secondary label color (Subheadline/Callout); time labels = 10–11 pt using `.monospacedDigit()` in tertiary color. Live Activity guidance says to use "medium weight or higher" on black, so the title should be semibold, not regular. Truncate with `.lineLimit(1)` and `.truncationMode(.tail)`. Apple Music on iOS 26 scrolls (marquees) long titles, but static truncation is the restrained choice.
- **[Estimate] Artwork:** The Mac menu bar / Control Center Now Playing tile uses small square artwork, roughly 40–56 pt, with a small continuous corner radius of roughly 6–10 pt. The Mac Music player bar's thumbnail is roughly 40–48 pt with a radius of roughly 4–6 pt. Apple's convention is a corner radius of about 10–20% of the artwork's side; larger art gets a larger absolute radius, as in the full player. For a notch module, the documented concentric rule gives the radius: inner radius = outer container radius − padding. Example: a container with a 20 pt radius and 8 pt padding gives a 12 pt artwork radius. Use `RoundedRectangle(cornerRadius:, style: .continuous)`, or `ConcentricRectangle` / `.containerShape` on macOS 26+.
- **[Estimate] Scrubber:** Apple's Now Playing scrubbers (iOS Lock Screen / Control Center, Mac Music player bar) use a thin capsule track of roughly 3–4 pt at rest. The track gets taller, roughly 6–8 pt, and the thumb appears only while hovering or dragging. The filled part is primary (white on dark) and the remaining track is a low-opacity white. Elapsed time sits at the leading end and remaining time ("−1:23") at the trailing end, both small and monospaced.
- **[Estimate] Controls:** Compact surfaces show only previous / play-pause / next. Play-pause is visually larger, roughly 1.3–1.5× the skip glyphs. In a compact module the skip glyphs are roughly 13–17 pt and play-pause roughly 18–22 pt, with no button backgrounds (plain/borderless glyphs).
- The MiniPlayer's hover-reveal pattern (art at rest, controls and title on hover) suits a notch HUD's collapsed-to-expanded behavior. The 26.2 change that hid the title at rest drew complaints, so keep at least the title visible in any non-collapsed state.

### Gaps
- No source found with exact pixel or point dimensions for the MiniPlayer, the Control Center Now Playing tile, artwork radius, scrubber height, or control glyph sizes on macOS 26. The full Apple Support, 9to5Mac, Six Colors, and MacStories pages could not be fetched. A measurement pass on real Tahoe screenshots, using Xcode's Accessibility Inspector or pixel-measuring a screenshot, is needed to confirm the [Estimate] values.

## 2. Apple TV app (macOS/iOS 26): card/poster radii, typography, focus/hover, restraint

### Takeaway
In the 26 releases, the TV app on Mac, iPhone, and Apple TV moved from landscape to vertical (portrait) poster art, showing more titles at once, with Liquid Glass chrome. The restraint principles that apply: content (artwork) carries the color, and chrome stays neutral. Nested artwork should use concentric corners. Tint should be used only for a single primary action.

### Cited Findings
- The Apple TV app is "redesigned with beautiful new poster art that incorporates Liquid Glass" and shows more shows and movies at once. [Reported via snippet of Apple Newsroom and Apple's macOS Tahoe features PDF] — [Apple Newsroom: Apple TV redesign](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2025/06/apple-tv-brings-a-beautiful-redesign-and-enhanced-home-entertainment-experience/); [New features in macOS Tahoe (PDF)](https://www.apple.com/os/pdf/All_New_Features_macOS_Tahoe_Sept_2025.pdf)
- New vertical poster art replaces landscape artwork in lists across iOS 26 and tvOS 26, so content density goes up. The player controls were also redesigned. [Reported] — [9to5Mac, Jul 2025](https://9to5mac.com/2025/07/16/apples-tv-app-gets-fresh-design-in-ios-26-and-tvos-26-heres-whats-new/); [AppleInsider tvOS 26 hands-on](https://appleinsider.com/articles/25/06/18/tvos-26-hands-on-sleek-liquid-glass-redesign-new-control-center-and-more)
- Concentricity [Documented, WWDC25]: "We use three shape types… fixed shapes have a constant corner radius. Capsules use a radius that's half the height of the container. And concentric shapes calculate their radius by subtracting padding from the parent's." The talk also says to watch for "corners that feel too pinched — or flared… nested containers—like artwork in a card… Its shape probably needs to be concentric." — [WWDC25 "Get to know the new design system"](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/356/)
- Tinting restraint [Documented, WWDC25]: "Avoid tinting all your elements. When every element is tinted, nothing stands out… If you want to imbue color into your app, do it in the content layer instead." A primary action gets tint, as in the example of a red "View Bag" button. — [WWDC25 "Meet Liquid Glass"](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/219/)
- HIG for video: "Use the system video player… If your app truly requires a custom video player, reference the behavior and interface of the system video player"; "people expect to press Space… to play or pause media playback." — [HIG: Playing video](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/playing-video)

### Inferences
- **[Estimate]** TV app posters on Mac use a modest continuous corner radius, roughly 8–12 pt on cards about 150–200 pt wide. Hovering on Mac brings a subtle lift or scale (around 1.02–1.05×) and a shadow, rather than tvOS-style large focus parallax. I could not verify either value.
- The design lesson for a notch module: let the artwork be the only colorful element. Text and controls stay white or gray, and no element gets tinted except possibly one "active" state (for example, AirPlay route active).

### Gaps
- No documented Mac TV app poster corner radius or hover spec was found. The 9to5Mac and AppleInsider pages could not be fetched.

## 3. What changed in macOS 26 Tahoe / iOS 26 (Liquid Glass); solid vs translucent; flat solid equivalents

### Takeaway
Liquid Glass is a translucent, adaptive "functional layer" for controls and navigation that floats above an opaque content layer. Artwork and content stay solid. Bars, control platters, sidebars, the menu bar, and Control Center are glass. Apple's own fallbacks show what a flat version looks like: Reduce Transparency makes the glass frostier, Increase Contrast makes elements predominantly black or white with a contrasting border, and the Dynamic Island's black opaque background is Apple's documented solid-black precedent. A no-glass notch module should therefore be a solid black container holding solid artwork, white primary text, gray secondary text, and white glyphs, with no tinted platters.

### Cited Findings
- "Liquid Glass layers form a distinct functional layer for controls and navigation, floating above everything"; "Glass controls nest perfectly into the rounded corners of windows, maintaining concentricity." Each layer "continuously adapts based on what's behind it," and the material "can also independently switch between light and dark." — [WWDC25 "Meet Liquid Glass"](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/219/)
- "In steady states… avoid intersections between content and Liquid Glass." Also: "Here is a button that is using a solid fill instead of the built-in tinting. As you can tell, it is completely opaque and breaks the visual character of Liquid Glass." — [WWDC25 219](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/219/)
- Accessibility modifiers [Documented]: "Reduced Transparency makes Liquid Glass frostier and obscures more of the content behind it. Increased contrast makes elements predominantly black or white and highlights them with a contrasting border, and Reduced Motion decreases the intensity of some effects and disables any elastic properties." — [WWDC25 219](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/219/)
- macOS sidebars are now inset and built with Liquid Glass, and content can extend behind them. — [WWDC25 356](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/356/)
- iOS 26 Music: album art gets a full-screen Lock Screen design that can animate, "with playback controls resting on a platter of a liquid glass." The `MPMediaItemAnimatedArtwork` API opens this to third parties. [Reported] — [9to5Mac, Jun 2025](https://9to5mac.com/2025/06/11/apple-music-brings-your-lock-screen-to-life-with-new-ios-26-look/); API: [MPMediaItemAnimatedArtwork docs](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/mediaplayer/mpmediaitemanimatedartwork) [Documented: "an animated image, such as an animated music album cover art… comprised of two assets: an artwork video asset, and a preview image which should match the first frame"]
- iOS 26 Music: the song title "ticks along like a stock-market ticker." iOS 26.1 added swipe left/right on the mini-player to skip tracks. iOS 26.1 also added a Clear vs Tinted Liquid Glass setting under Display & Brightness. [Reported via snippets] — [MacRumors iOS 26 roundup](https://www.macrumors.com/roundup/ios-26/); [NN/g critique](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/liquid-glass/)
- Tahoe criticism: the Music control bar is transparent and loses contrast over white content and scrolling art. [Reported] — [Apple Community 256142581](https://discussions.apple.com/thread/256142581); [MacRumors Tahoe review thread](https://forums.macrumors.com/threads/macos-tahoe-review-spotlight-shines-liquid-glass-disappoints.2462642/)
- **Newer (flag):** macOS 27 "Golden Gate" (announced at WWDC 2026, released September 2026) adds a user slider for Liquid Glass transparency vs opacity. Reports say the changes are small and the pre-Tahoe design does not return. I found no Music or Now Playing-specific changes for macOS 27. [Reported via snippets] — [AppleInsider, Jun 2026](https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/06/09/dont-get-excited-for-big-liquid-glass-changes-in-macos-27-because-they-arent-there); [MacRumors macOS 27 features](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/06/04/macos-27-features/); [Six Colors macOS 27 review](https://sixcolors.com/post/2026/09/macos-27-golden-gate-review-bridging-the-tahoe-gap/)

### Inferences
- **Solid vs translucent in Apple's media surfaces (26):** solid/opaque elements are the artwork, video, text glyphs, and the progress fill. Translucent elements are the Music player bar, the iOS Now Playing control platter, Control Center and its modules, the menu bar, sidebars, and the mini-player tab accessory.
- **Flat equivalent for this project (no gradients, blur, or glass):**
  - Container: pure `Color.black`, which matches both the notch and the documented Dynamic Island black opaque background.
  - Primary text: white (`.primary` in a dark color scheme).
  - Secondary text: white at about 55–60% opacity. Tertiary (time labels, disabled glyphs): white at about 30–40%. These are [Estimate]s matching the opacities of `secondaryLabel`/`tertiaryLabel` in dark mode. Use `.foregroundStyle(.secondary)` / `.tertiary` with `.environment(\.colorScheme, .dark)` so the system values carry through.
  - Scrubber: remaining track is white at about 20% and the fill is white, both solid.
  - Hover backgrounds: a solid white at 8–12% opacity, either a capsule or a concentric rounded rectangle. This replaces Apple's glass "illuminate from within" feedback.
  - Increase Contrast: raise secondary text to about 80% and add a 1 pt white hairline border. This mirrors Apple's documented Increase Contrast behavior of predominantly black/white with a contrasting border.
- **Artwork-derived color:** In the 26 releases Apple puts artwork color into the *content* layer: the full-screen and animated Lock Screen art, and the colored background of the Music full player. The chrome is not tinted, and WWDC guidance says to keep color in the content layer. A flat, no-gradient notch module should not wash the background with artwork color. The artwork itself provides the color. If you want any accent at all, use a single solid color sampled from the artwork, for example on the scrubber fill. That is optional and not something Apple does in compact surfaces [Estimate].
- **Empty state [Estimate, unverified]:** Apple's Control Center / menu bar Now Playing shows "Not Playing" in secondary text when nothing has played, with a gray placeholder tile. The Music app's placeholder is a gray square with a `music.note` glyph. If an app has paused but is still the Now Playing app, the module keeps showing the last track with a play glyph. Recommended for a notch: a 2–3 step neutral gray square (for example, white at 8% on black) with `music.note` at tertiary opacity, plus "Not Playing" in secondary text. Alternatively, hide the module entirely, which matches the "Show When Active" option.

### Gaps
- Could not read Apple Newsroom, Six Colors, or MacStories in full, so per-element descriptions of the Tahoe Music full player, such as whether the full-screen player uses an artwork-color background on Mac, are unverified.

## 4. SF Symbols used for playback/AirPlay and their rendering

### Takeaway
Apple's transport glyphs are the filled variants: `backward.fill`, `play.fill` / `pause.fill`, and `forward.fill`. The AirPlay audio glyph is `airplay.audio` in current SF Symbols; the older name is `airplayaudio`. They are rendered monochrome as plain glyphs on a neutral surface, with play-pause larger than the skip glyphs. The weights and point sizes Apple uses are not documented.

### Cited Findings
- `backward.fill` and `forward.fill` are the SF Symbols used for media skip controls, and `airplayaudio` is the AirPlay audio symbol. [Reported, secondary] — [Pyto sf_symbols list](https://pyto.readthedocs.io/en/latest/library/sf_symbols.html); [Cult of Mac: Apple Music icons explained](https://www.cultofmac.com/how-to/apple-music-signs-symbols-icons)
- Tahoe/iOS 26 bar guidance: group related symbol buttons, "don't group symbols with text," and keep a primary action separate and tinted. — [WWDC25 356](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/356/)
- The HIG SF Symbols page covers rendering modes (monochrome, hierarchical, palette, multicolor), weights matched to adjacent text, and symbol scales (small/medium/large). — [HIG: SF Symbols](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sf-symbols)

### Inferences
- **[Estimate] Symbol names to use:** `backward.fill`, `play.fill`, `pause.fill`, `forward.fill`, and `airplay.audio` (fall back to `airplayaudio` before SF Symbols 6). Also `speaker.wave.2.fill` (volume), `quote.bubble` (lyrics, as in Music), `list.bullet` (queue), `music.note` (placeholder), `shuffle`, `repeat`, and `repeat.1`. Use `.contentTransition(.symbolEffect(.replace))` for the play↔pause swap. It is a cross-fade/morph that respects Reduce Motion.
- **[Estimate] Rendering:** monochrome, white, semibold to bold weight (Apple's Now Playing transport glyphs look heavier than body text). Use `.imageScale(.large)` or an explicit `.font(.system(size:, weight: .semibold))`. A suggested compact set: skip glyphs at 14–16 pt, play/pause at 20–22 pt, and AirPlay at 13–14 pt in secondary opacity. AirPlay turns accent-colored (blue) only when routed to an external device, matching iOS. Hierarchical rendering is not needed for these single-layer glyphs.
- Give each glyph a hit target of about 28×28 pt or larger, since the Mac minimum is around 20–28 pt [Estimate]. Hover feedback should be a circular solid fill behind the glyph (white at about 10%) and/or raising glyph opacity from secondary to primary. Pressed state: scale to about 0.9.

### Gaps
- No Apple primary source confirms the exact weights or point sizes of transport glyphs in Music or Control Center. I also did not confirm the official `airplay.audio` rename date; check it in the SF Symbols app.

## 5. Accessibility behaviors (VoiceOver, Reduce Motion, transparency/contrast)

### Takeaway
Apple's documented accessibility levers for these surfaces are Reduce Transparency, Increase Contrast, and Reduce Motion, all applied as Liquid Glass modifiers, plus the expectation that Space plays and pauses. A custom notch module should follow the same rules: system labels on controls, a combined element for the track info, adjustable scrubber semantics, and no bouncy or elastic motion when Reduce Motion is on.

### Cited Findings
- Reduce Transparency / Increase Contrast / Reduce Motion behavior for Liquid Glass (quoted in §3). — [WWDC25 219](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/219/)
- "Avoid communicating important information using only sound" (visionOS note, a general principle). Also: "People expect to press Space… to play or pause." — [HIG: Playing audio](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/playing-audio); [HIG: Playing video](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/playing-video)
- "Avoid repurposing audio controls… If your app doesn't support certain controls, don't respond to them." — [HIG: Playing audio](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/playing-audio)
- Live Activities: "Verify your design in Night Mode… ensure sufficient contrast — especially for tint colors… with reduced luminance." — [HIG: Live Activities](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/live-activities)
- AppleVis has a Tahoe-specific accessibility changes and bugs write-up (not fetched). — [AppleVis macOS Tahoe](https://www.applevis.com/blog/macos-tahoe-new-features-changes-improvements-bugs-blind-deafblind-low-vision-users)

### Inferences
- **[Estimate] VoiceOver:**
  - Labels: "Previous", "Play" / "Pause" (the label changes with state), "Next", and "AirPlay".
  - Track info: combine into one element with `.accessibilityElement(children: .combine)`, reading "Title, Artist".
  - Scrubber: make it `.accessibilityAdjustableAction`, with a value like "1:23 of 3:45".
  - Hover-only controls: they must stay reachable by VoiceOver and the keyboard. Do not use opacity 0 in a way that removes them from the accessibility tree.
- **Reduce Motion:** replace slide or spring expansion with a cross-fade, disable title marquee scrolling, and avoid elastic overshoot (Apple disables "elastic properties" under Reduce Motion).
- **Differentiate Without Color / Increase Contrast:** don't rely on artwork color for state. Check `accessibilityDisplayShouldIncreaseContrast` and raise text opacities.

### Gaps
- I found no Apple documentation listing the exact VoiceOver strings used by Music or Control Center Now Playing on macOS. The labels above are conventions, not verified strings.
